Level Systems

Level System are a Progression that sacrifice your experience points into Level Points, Reaching max required points will Level Up and gain 1 Points

Usage

Clicking "+" sacrifice some points and added to level xp, reaching required or 100% will level up gaining 1 Points

Attributes

Strenght

    • Gain 0.5 Attack Damage per points added

    • Agility

    • Gain 0.01% Attack Speed per points added

    • Dexerity

    • Reduce fall damage by 1% per points
      Gain 0.001% Movement Speed per points added
